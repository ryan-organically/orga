Frequently Asked Questions

How much does software product development cost? +
It depends entirely on scope, complexity, and timeline. A simple MVP might cost $25,000-$75,000 and take 2-3 months. A full-featured SaaS product could be $150,000-$500,000+ over 6-12 months. We provide detailed estimates after understanding your specific requirements. We're always transparent about costs and work with you to prioritize features that fit your budget.
How long does it take to build a software product? +
A focused MVP can be ready in 6-12 weeks. A more comprehensive initial product typically takes 3-6 months. Complex enterprise solutions or feature-rich platforms may take 9-12+ months. We prefer to launch early with core features and iterate based on user feedback rather than building everything upfront.
Do you work with non-technical founders? +
Absolutely. Many of our best partnerships are with non-technical founders who have deep domain expertise but need a technical partner. We explain technical concepts in plain English, help you make informed decisions, and never use jargon to obscure what we're doing. Your job is to understand your users and market. Ours is to build the technology.
Who owns the code and intellectual property? +
You do. 100%. All code, designs, documentation, and intellectual property belong to you from day one. We don't retain any ownership rights. You'll have full access to all repositories and can take the code elsewhere if needed. We want to earn your continued business through quality, not lock you in through ownership games.
What happens after launch? +
Launch is just the beginning. We offer ongoing maintenance, support, and continued development. Most clients transition to a retainer model where we handle bug fixes, security updates, performance optimization, and new feature development. We can scale up for major releases or scale down during quieter periods.
Can you help us hire our own development team? +
Yes. We've helped multiple clients transition from working with us to building their internal team. We can help define roles, participate in technical interviews, onboard new hires, and ensure knowledge transfer. Our goal is your long-term success, even if that means eventually replacing us.
