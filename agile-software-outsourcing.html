1

Our agile outsourcing teams work hours that overlap with your core business hours. Minimum 4-hour overlap for US clients, more for European and Asian timezones. Real-time collaboration, not async handoffs.

2

Your Tools, Your Channels

We integrate into your existing workflow. Slack, Teams, Discord - whatever you use. Our developers appear in your channels alongside your internal team. No separate communication silos.

3

Video-First Culture

Cameras on for standups and meetings. You know who you are working with. Building rapport and trust is impossible through text alone. Our team becomes your team, faces included.

4

Every architectural decision, scope change, and technical tradeoff gets documented. When questions arise months later, the context is preserved. No tribal knowledge that walks out the door.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is agile outsourcing different from traditional outsourcing? +
Traditional outsourcing is waterfall in disguise. You write a spec, hand it over, and hope for the best. Agile software development outsourcing means iterative delivery, continuous collaboration, and the ability to change direction based on what you learn. You are involved in every sprint, not just at kickoff and delivery.
What timezone do your developers work in? +
We match developers to your timezone requirements. For US clients, we guarantee minimum 4-hour overlap with your business hours. For projects requiring more real-time collaboration, we can provide developers with even greater overlap. This is non-negotiable for effective agile outsourcing.
Can your team work with our existing internal developers? +
Absolutely. Most of our agile software outsourcing engagements involve augmenting existing teams. Our developers integrate into your workflows, follow your coding standards, and participate in your ceremonies. From your internal team's perspective, they are just additional team members.
How quickly can you scale the team up or down? +
Adding developers typically takes 1-2 weeks for proper onboarding. Scaling down can happen at the end of any sprint with 30 days notice. We plan for flexibility because we know priorities change. That is the point of agile.
What if we want to bring development in-house eventually? +
That is a success story, not an exit. We document everything, maintain clean code, and help you hire when you are ready. Some clients even hire developers who worked on their projects. Our goal is building your capability, not creating dependency.
How do you handle security and IP protection? +
All developers sign NDAs before starting. We support SOC2 compliant workflows, work within your VPN if required, and can operate under your security policies. You own all code and IP from day one. Our agile software outsourcing contracts are structured to protect your interests.
