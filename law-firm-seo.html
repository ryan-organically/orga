Our Approach to Legal SEO

Built for How Clients Actually Find Lawyers

People searching for attorneys aren't browsing. They have problems—accidents, divorces, criminal charges, business disputes. They need help now, and they're looking for someone they can trust.

We build SEO strategies around this reality:

Intent-Focused Keywords: We target searches that lead to consultations, not just traffic. "Car accident lawyer [city]" converts better than "what to do after a car accident."

Local Dominance: Most legal searches are local. We optimize your Google Business Profile, build local citations, and create location-specific content that ranks in the map pack.

Authority Building: Google holds legal content to higher standards (YMYL). We help you demonstrate expertise through comprehensive content, proper credentials display, and strategic link building.

Conversion Tracking: We track what matters—phone calls, form submissions, consultations booked, cases signed. Not just rankings.
