Frequently Asked Questions

How long does custom ERP development take? +
Timeline depends on complexity and scope. A focused ERP module might take 3-4 months, while a comprehensive enterprise system could require 6-12 months. We typically work in phases, delivering usable functionality early and iterating based on feedback. During our initial consultation, we will provide a realistic timeline based on your specific requirements.
Should we build a custom ERP or use off-the-shelf software? +
It depends on your business. Off-the-shelf ERP works well for companies with standard processes who can adapt to the software's workflow. Custom development makes sense when you have unique processes that create competitive advantage, when licensing costs for your user count become prohibitive, or when you need integrations that pre-built software does not support. We can help you evaluate both options.
What is the cost of custom ERP development? +
Custom ERP development costs vary significantly based on scope, complexity, and integration requirements. A single-module solution might start around $50,000, while comprehensive enterprise systems can exceed $500,000. However, custom ERP often provides better long-term ROI than subscription software with ongoing per-user fees. We provide detailed estimates after understanding your requirements.
Can you integrate with our existing systems? +
Yes. ERP integration services are a core part of what Organically does. We build connections to CRM systems, e-commerce platforms, accounting software, payment processors, shipping providers, and custom applications. If the system has an API or database access, we can integrate it. We also build custom APIs when needed.
What support do you provide after launch? +
We offer ongoing maintenance and support packages that include bug fixes, security updates, performance optimization, and feature enhancements. Most clients start with a support agreement after launch, then transition to as-needed development as the system stabilizes. Your ERP is an investment, and we are committed to keeping it running smoothly.
Why choose Odoo for ERP development? +
Odoo offers a modular, open-source foundation with thousands of pre-built apps. This means faster development time for common functionality while still allowing deep customization. The open-source model eliminates vendor lock-in and reduces licensing costs. As experienced Odoo developers, Organically helps businesses leverage the platform for the best balance of speed, flexibility, and cost.
