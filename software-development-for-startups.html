From Idea to Launch

Our startup development process is designed for speed and learning. Here is how we turn your vision into a shipped product.

1

Discovery Call

We start with a free 30-minute call to understand your vision, target users, and timeline. No sales pitch, just honest conversation about whether Organically is the right fit for your startup software development needs.

2

Scope and Proposal

We define the MVP scope together, identifying the core features that validate your hypothesis. You get a detailed proposal with timeline, cost, and tech stack recommendations. No hidden fees, no scope creep surprises.

3

Design Sprint

We design the user experience and create interactive prototypes you can test with real users before we write code. This catches UX problems early when they are cheap to fix.

4

Build in Sprints

Development happens in 2-week sprints with demos at the end of each. You see progress constantly and can adjust priorities as you learn. No disappearing for months only to emerge with something that missed the mark.

5

Launch and Iterate

We deploy to production with proper monitoring and analytics. Then we stick around to fix issues, iterate based on user feedback, and help you plan the next phase. Your startup app development journey is just beginning.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does startup software development cost? +
It depends on scope. A focused MVP typically runs $15K-$50K. A full product with mobile apps, admin dashboards, and integrations can be $75K-$200K+. We always start with a free consultation to understand your needs and give you a realistic estimate. The worst thing we can do is underprice and under-deliver, so we are honest about what things cost.
How long does it take to build an MVP? +
Most MVPs take 4-8 weeks from kickoff to launch. This includes design, development, and deployment. The key is ruthless scope management. We help you identify the absolute minimum feature set needed to validate your hypothesis. Anything beyond that is v1.1.
Do you work with non-technical founders? +
Absolutely. Most of Organically's clients are non-technical founders. We act as your technical co-founder alternative, translating technical decisions into business terms so you can make informed choices. You do not need to know what a database is to build a successful startup. You need to know your customers and market. We handle the rest.
Can you help us find a technical co-founder instead? +
Yes. Sometimes that is the right path. We can help you evaluate whether you need a technical co-founder services relationship or if working with a development agency makes more sense for your situation. Check out our guide to finding a technical co-founder for more context.
What happens after you build our MVP? +
You own everything. If you want to take the code and run, you can. Most clients continue working with Organically as they iterate and scale. When you are ready to build an internal engineering team, we help you hire and onboard your first developers. Our goal is to make ourselves unnecessary, not to create dependency.
Do you work with idea-stage startups? +
Yes, but we will push back if you are not ready. Building software is expensive. Before we write code, we want to see that you have talked to potential customers, have some hypothesis about why they will pay for your solution, and are committed to the startup journey. A 30-minute call helps us figure out if now is the right time to build.
