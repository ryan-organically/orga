## Anchor Text Guidelines

### Internal Links - Use Natural, Descriptive Anchor Text:
✅ GOOD:
- "Check out our SEO services for electricians"
- "Learn more about electrician SEO strategies"
- "Read our complete guide to YouTube optimization"
- "See how restaurants can improve local rankings"

❌ AVOID:
- "Click here"
- "Read more"
- "Link"
- Over-optimized exact match (e.g., "electrician SEO electrician SEO")

### External Links - Use Contextual, Relevant Anchor Text:
✅ GOOD:
- "According to Google's Mobile-Friendly Test"
- "The National Electrical Contractors Association recommends"
- "Use tools like Ahrefs to research keywords"
- "YouTube Creator Academy provides video best practices"

---

## Link Placement Best Practices

1. **Contextual Links**: Place links naturally within content where they add value
2. **Early Placement**: Include important links in the first 1-2 paragraphs when possible
3. **CTA Links**: Always include service page CTAs at the end of blog posts
4. **Related Content**: Add "Related Articles" section at bottom of blog posts
5. **Navigation**: Ensure all pages are accessible from main navigation or footer
6. **Breadcrumbs**: Implement breadcrumb navigation for better UX and SEO

---

## Notes

- All internal links should use relative URLs (e.g., `/seo-for-electricians` not full URL)
- External links should use `rel="noopener"` for security
- Avoid linking to direct competitors
- Prioritize .gov, .edu, and industry association links for authority
- Update link strategy quarterly as new content is created
