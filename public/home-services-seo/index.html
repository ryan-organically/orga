Competing with HomeAdvisor, Angi, and Pay-Per-Lead Traps

Home service companies face a brutal customer acquisition problem. Lead generation sites sell the same lead to multiple contractors, forcing you to race to call first and compete on price.

The pay-per-lead trap:

- Same lead sold to 3-5 competitors
- Rising costs as more contractors join
- No compound value—stop paying, stop getting leads
- Customers trained to shop on price, not quality
- Low-intent leads that waste your time

SEO offers an escape: leads that come directly to you, no sharing, no per-lead fees, compounding value over time.

Own Your Leads with Organic Search

When you rank #1 for "AC repair near me" or "roofing company [city]," those customers call you first—and only you.

The SEO advantage:

- Exclusive leads that come directly to you
- Customers call because they found YOU
- Investment compounds over time
- Rankings get stronger, not weaker
- Cost per lead decreases as rankings improve
- Competitive moat that's hard to replicate
