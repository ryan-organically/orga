Why Your Practice Isn't Ranking Locally

The dentist ranking #1 in your area isn't necessarily better at dentistry. They're better at being found. Local SEO for dental practices requires specialized tactics that generic SEO agencies don't understand—and that most dental marketing companies get wrong.

Google Business Profile Optimization

Your Google Business Profile is the single most important asset for local dental SEO. But most practices have incomplete profiles with generic descriptions, missing services, outdated hours, and zero posts. Google rewards active, complete profiles with higher Map Pack placement.

We optimize every field: primary and secondary categories, service areas, attributes (wheelchair accessible, accepts new patients), Q&A, photos with geo-tags, and weekly posts that signal to Google your practice is active and engaged.

The Review Velocity Problem

A practice with 50 reviews from 3 years ago ranks worse than a practice with 30 reviews from the last 6 months. Google measures review velocity—how fast new reviews come in—as a trust signal. If your competitor gets 5 reviews per month and you get 1, they'll outrank you even with a lower overall rating.

We implement review generation systems that work within Google's guidelines: post-appointment SMS sequences, email follow-ups, and staff training so asking for reviews becomes part of your checkout process.

Local SEO Strategy for Dental Practices

Local SEO isn't "regular SEO but for local businesses." It's a distinct discipline with its own ranking factors, tactics, and pitfalls.

NAP Consistency Across 100+ Directories

NAP stands for Name, Address, Phone number. Google cross-references your practice information across hundreds of directories—Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yellow Pages, local chamber listings, and more. Any inconsistency (different suite numbers, old phone numbers, misspellings) creates confusion that tanks your rankings.

We audit every citation, correct inconsistencies, and build new citations on high-authority directories that specifically impact dental local search. This foundational work often produces ranking improvements within 60-90 days.

Location-Specific Service Pages

A generic "Services" page won't rank for local searches. You need dedicated pages for each service in each location you serve. "Emergency Dentist in [Your City]" with 1,500+ words of unique content outranks a thin page every time.

We create service-location content clusters: "Dental Implants in [City]," "Teeth Whitening in [City]," "Invisalign Provider in [City]"—each optimized for local intent keywords, embedded with schema markup, and internally linked to support your site's topical authority.

Local Link Building That Actually Moves Rankings

Generic backlink packages from overseas SEO farms will get you penalized. Local dental SEO requires links from locally-relevant sources: your city's chamber of commerce, local news coverage, dental association directories, community organization sponsorships, and partnerships with complementary local businesses.

We focus on quality over quantity—earning links that signal to Google "this practice is an established, trusted part of this community."

What Sets Dental Local SEO Apart

Dental-Specific Search Intent

Patients searching for a dentist have different intent patterns than other local searches. "Dentist near me" is often an emergency—someone with a cracked tooth or sudden pain who needs to book today. "Best dentist for veneers" is research-stage, comparison shopping. "Pediatric dentist accepting new patients [city]" is ready to book.

Your local SEO strategy must capture all three: emergency keywords for immediate appointments, research keywords for consideration-stage patients, and transactional keywords for patients ready to schedule.

Insurance and Payment Keywords

Patients search by insurance provider: "Delta Dental dentist near me," "dentist that takes Cigna [city]." If your website doesn't clearly state which insurance plans you accept—and have dedicated pages for each major provider—you're invisible to these searches.

We build insurance-specific landing pages that capture this traffic and convert it into appointments with patients who already know you're in-network.

Mobile-First, Click-to-Call Optimization

78% of local searches on mobile result in an offline action within 24 hours. Your website must be optimized for mobile-first indexing, with prominent click-to-call buttons, easy-to-find address and hours, and fast load times. Slow, clunky mobile experiences don't just frustrate patients—they actively hurt your rankings.

Transparent Reporting, No Vanity Metrics

We report on what matters: Map Pack position, phone calls, appointment requests, and new patient acquisitions. Not "impressions" or "reach" that don't translate to revenue. Every month you'll see exactly how your local visibility is improving and which efforts are driving results.
