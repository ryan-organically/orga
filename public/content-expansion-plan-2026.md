## Part 4: Content Templates

### Service Page Template (Primary)
```
1. Hero Section
   - H1: [Service] - [Value Prop]
   - Subhead: Benefit-driven statement
   - CTA: Contact/Get Quote

2. Problem Statement (100-150 words)
   - Industry challenges
   - Why custom solutions matter

3. Our Services (400-500 words)
   - Service components (H3s)
   - Feature lists
   - Tech stack badges

4. Process Overview (300-400 words)
   - 4-6 step process
   - Timeline indicators

5. Industry Expertise (200-300 words)
   - Relevant verticals
   - Case study previews

6. Technology Stack (200 words)
   - Icons/badges
   - Brief descriptions

7. Why Choose Us (250-300 words)
   - 4-6 differentiators
   - Statistics if available

8. FAQ Section (300-400 words)
   - 5-7 questions
   - Schema markup

9. CTA Section
   - Contact form
   - Meeting scheduler
```

### Industry Vertical Template
```
1. Hero with industry imagery
2. Industry challenges (specific pain points)
3. Compliance/regulations section (if applicable)
4. Solution features for this industry
5. Case studies/portfolio
6. Technology expertise
7. FAQ (industry-specific)
8. CTA
```

### Comparison/Informational Template
```
1. Definition/Overview
2. Key considerations
3. Comparison table
4. Pros/Cons
5. How to choose
6. Our approach
7. CTA
```
