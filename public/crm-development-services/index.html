Frequently Asked Questions

How long does custom CRM development take? +
Timeline depends on complexity. A basic custom CRM with core features typically takes 3-4 months. More complex enterprise solutions with extensive integrations and custom workflows can take 6-12 months. We provide detailed timelines during the discovery phase after understanding your specific requirements. We use agile methodology, so you will see working features every 2 weeks throughout the project.
Should I build a custom CRM or use Salesforce/HubSpot? +
It depends on your needs. Off-the-shelf platforms like Salesforce and HubSpot are excellent for most businesses—they are battle-tested, regularly updated, and have extensive ecosystems. Custom CRM development makes sense when: (1) your workflows are truly unique and cannot be replicated in existing platforms, (2) you need complete control over your data and infrastructure, (3) per-user licensing costs are prohibitive at scale, or (4) you require deep integration with proprietary systems. We will give you an honest assessment during our consultation.
How much does CRM development cost? +
CRM development costs vary widely based on scope. Salesforce or HubSpot customization projects typically range from $5,000-$50,000 depending on complexity. Fully custom CRM development starts around $30,000 for basic systems and can exceed $200,000 for enterprise solutions with extensive features and integrations. We provide detailed proposals with fixed pricing after the discovery phase—no surprise costs or scope creep.
Will my team need training on the new CRM? +
Yes, and we include training as part of every project. A CRM is only valuable if people actually use it. We provide documentation, video tutorials, and live training sessions tailored to different user roles. We also design with adoption in mind—intuitive interfaces, helpful tooltips, and workflows that match how your team already works. The goal is to make the CRM feel like a natural extension of their daily work, not another tool to learn.
What happens after the CRM is launched? +
We offer ongoing support and maintenance packages to ensure your CRM continues to perform. This includes bug fixes, security updates, performance monitoring, and minor enhancements. As your business evolves, we can add new features, integrations, and workflows. Many clients retain us on a monthly basis for continuous improvement—your CRM should grow with your business, not become a static tool that falls behind your needs.
