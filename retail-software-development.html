Our Retail Software Development Process

Building custom retail software is a partnership. Here's how Organically works with retail clients to create solutions that actually solve real operational challenges.

01

Discovery & Strategy

We start by understanding your retail operations, pain points, and goals. We audit your existing systems, interview stakeholders, and map your customer journey. The output is a detailed requirements document and technical architecture plan.

02

Design & Prototyping

Before writing code, we design the user experience. Interactive prototypes let you test workflows and interfaces with real users. We iterate based on feedback until the design is right—saving expensive changes later.

03

Agile Development

We build in 2-week sprints with regular demos. You see progress constantly, provide feedback early, and can adjust priorities as needed. No surprises at the end—you're involved throughout the entire development process.

04

Testing & Launch

Rigorous testing across devices, browsers, and scenarios. We run pilot programs in select locations before full rollout. Training and documentation ensure your team is ready. Then we launch—and stay on call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to develop custom retail software? +
Development timelines vary based on complexity. A basic POS system might take 3-4 months, while a comprehensive omnichannel solution could take 6-12 months. We provide detailed timelines during our discovery phase after understanding your specific requirements. The good news: we build in phases, so you can start using core features while we continue developing additional capabilities.
Can you integrate with our existing systems? +
Yes, integration is a core part of our retail software development services. We build solutions that connect with your existing ERP (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite), accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero), e-commerce platforms (Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce), payment processors (Stripe, Square, Adyen), and third-party logistics providers. If it has an API, we can integrate with it.
Do you provide ongoing support after launch? +
Absolutely. Retail software requires continuous updates for security patches, new payment regulations, and feature enhancements. We offer flexible support packages ranging from basic maintenance to full managed services with 24/7 support. Most clients start with a standard support package and scale up as their operations grow. You own the code—we're here to help maintain and improve it.
What makes custom retail software better than off-the-shelf solutions? +
Custom retail software is built around your specific workflows, not the other way around. You get exactly the features you need, seamless integration with your existing systems, and the flexibility to adapt as your business grows—without paying for features you'll never use or being locked into a vendor's roadmap. For retailers with unique processes, multiple locations, or specific compliance requirements, custom development often delivers better ROI than forcing your business to fit a generic tool.
How much does retail software development cost? +
Costs depend on scope, complexity, and integrations required. A focused solution like a custom POS system might start around $50,000-$100,000, while a full omnichannel platform with inventory management could range from $150,000-$500,000+. We provide detailed quotes after our discovery phase, broken down by feature and phase. We can also structure projects to start with an MVP and expand over time to spread costs.
