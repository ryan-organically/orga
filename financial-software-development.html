Financial services software development demands rigorous security standards. At Organically, we build compliance into every line of code, not as an afterthought.

Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard compliance is non-negotiable for payment processing. We implement all 12 PCI-DSS requirements, from network security to access controls, ensuring your software handles cardholder data safely.

SOC 2 Type II

Demonstrate your commitment to security with SOC 2 certification. We design systems that meet trust service criteria for security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy from the ground up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does financial software development take? +
Timeline varies based on complexity and compliance requirements. A basic fintech MVP might take 3-4 months, while a full banking platform could take 12-18 months. During our discovery phase, we'll provide a detailed timeline with milestones. We use agile methodology, so you'll see working software every 2-3 weeks and can adjust priorities as we go.
What compliance certifications do you help achieve? +
We build software that meets PCI-DSS (all levels), SOC 2 Type I and II, GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA (for healthcare payments), and various banking regulations including AML/KYC requirements, BSA compliance, and state money transmitter regulations. We work with your compliance team and auditors to ensure all requirements are documented and met. Our code includes the audit trails and controls auditors expect to see.
Can you integrate with existing banking systems? +
Absolutely. Integration is often the most challenging part of financial software development. We have experience integrating with core banking systems (FIS, Fiserv, Jack Henry), payment networks (SWIFT, ACH, FedWire, card networks), market data providers (Bloomberg, Refinitiv), and aggregation services (Plaid, Yodlee, MX). We can work with legacy systems, modern APIs, or build middleware to bridge the gap.
How do you handle data security during development? +
We never use real customer data in development or testing environments. All development happens in isolated environments with synthetic data that mirrors production patterns. Access is controlled via role-based permissions and logged for audit purposes. Source code is stored in encrypted repositories with branch protection. Every team member undergoes background checks and security training.
What ongoing support do you provide? +
Financial software requires continuous attention. We offer 24/7 monitoring and incident response, security patching and updates, compliance monitoring as regulations change, performance optimization, feature enhancements, and disaster recovery testing. Support plans are customized based on your uptime requirements and internal capabilities. Many clients start with full managed services and gradually bring operations in-house.
How much does financial software development cost? +
Costs depend heavily on scope, complexity, and compliance requirements. A basic fintech app might start around $150,000, while enterprise banking systems can reach into the millions. We provide detailed proposals after our discovery phase so you understand exactly what you're paying for. We can also phase projects to spread costs over time, starting with an MVP and adding features as your business grows.
