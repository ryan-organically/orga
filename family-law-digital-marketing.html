Family Law Digital Marketing: How to Attract Divorce and Custody Clients Online

Ryan Scanlon - Updated January 2025

What You're Reading...
The Client Journey SEO for Family Law Web Design Paid Advertising Reputation Management

Family law clients are different from other legal clients. They're going through one of the most difficult periods of their lives—divorce, custody battles, domestic issues. They need an attorney they can trust, and they're searching online to find one.

Effective family law digital marketing requires understanding this emotional landscape. The firms that succeed aren't the ones shouting the loudest—they're the ones who demonstrate competence, empathy, and trustworthiness when potential clients need it most.

76% of people seeking a divorce attorney start their search online. Your digital presence is often your first impression with clients during one of the most vulnerable moments of their lives.

The Family Law Client Journey Online

How Divorce and Custody Clients Search

Family law searches follow a distinct pattern:

Early Stage (Information Gathering):
- "How does divorce work in [state]"
- "Do I need a lawyer for divorce"
- "How long does divorce take"
- "What happens to the house in a divorce"

Middle Stage (Solution Seeking):
- "Divorce lawyer [city]"
- "Best family law attorney near me"
- "Child custody lawyer [city]"
- "How much does a divorce lawyer cost"

Decision Stage (Ready to Hire):
- "[Law firm name] reviews"
- "Free consultation divorce attorney [city]"
- "Family lawyer near me open now"

Emotional vs. Informational Search Intent

Family law searches often carry heavy emotional weight. Someone searching "how to protect kids in divorce" isn't just seeking information—they're scared, stressed, and looking for reassurance.

Your content should address both dimensions:

- Provide accurate, helpful legal information
- Acknowledge the emotional difficulty of their situation
- Demonstrate you understand what they're going through
- Offer clear next steps without pressure

SEO for Family Law Attorneys

High-Intent Keywords for Family Lawyers

Focus your SEO efforts on keywords that indicate readiness to hire:

Divorce Keywords:
- "Divorce attorney [city]"
- "Divorce lawyer near me"
- "Uncontested divorce lawyer [city]"
- "High net worth divorce attorney"

Custody Keywords:
- "Child custody lawyer [city]"
- "Custody modification attorney"
- "Father's rights lawyer [city]"
- "Emergency custody attorney"

Other Family Law Keywords:
- "Prenuptial agreement lawyer"
- "Child support attorney [city]"
- "Adoption lawyer near me"
- "Domestic violence attorney"

Local SEO for Divorce Attorneys

Most family law searches are local. Optimize for your geographic area:

Google Business Profile:
- Primary category: "Divorce Lawyer" or "Family Law Attorney"
- Add all practice areas as services
- Upload professional photos of your office and team
- Post regularly with helpful family law content
- Respond thoughtfully to all reviews

Local Content:
- Create pages for each city you serve
- Reference local courts and procedures
- Mention state-specific family law information
- Include local phone numbers

Content That Builds Trust During a Difficult Time

Family law content should educate while demonstrating empathy:

Educational Content:
- "The divorce process in [state]: What to expect"
- "How child custody is determined in [state]"
- "Dividing assets in a [state] divorce"
- "Your rights as a father in custody cases"

Supportive Content:
- "Preparing emotionally for divorce proceedings"
- "How to help children through a divorce"
- "What to look for in a family law attorney"
- "Questions to ask during your consultation"

Web Design for Family Lawyers

What Family Law Clients Need to See Immediately

Your website has seconds to make an impression. Family law clients need to immediately understand:

- You handle their type of case
- You practice in their area
- You're a real, qualified attorney
- How to contact you
- What the next step is

Above-the-fold elements:
- Clear headline stating your practice area and location
- Phone number (clickable on mobile)
- Professional photo of the attorney
- Clear call to action (free consultation)

Mobile Experience for Stressed, Searching Clients

Many family law searches happen on mobile, often during stressful moments. Your mobile experience must be flawless:

- Click-to-call phone number prominently displayed
- Easy-to-complete contact forms
- Fast loading speed (under 3 seconds)
- Easy navigation to key information
- Text readable without zooming

Intake Forms and Chat That Convert

Make it easy for potential clients to reach you:

Contact Forms:
- Keep forms short (name, phone, email, brief description)
- Don't require sensitive details upfront
- Confirm submission immediately
- Set expectations for response time

Live Chat:
- Offer chat for quick questions
- Staff with trained intake specialists (not chatbots for initial contact)
- Have clear after-hours protocols
- Connect to your CRM for follow-up

Paid Advertising for Family Law

Google Ads for Divorce Keywords

Google Ads can provide immediate visibility while building organic rankings:

Campaign Structure:
- Separate campaigns for different practice areas (divorce, custody, etc.)
- Location targeting to your service area
- Ad scheduling for business hours (when you can respond)
- Call extensions for direct phone contact

Keyword Strategy:
- Focus on high-intent keywords ([practice area] + lawyer/attorney + [location])
- Use negative keywords to avoid wasted spend
- Test different match types for optimal performance

Ad Copy:
- Emphasize free consultations if offered
- Include years of experience or case statistics
- Address common concerns (confidential, compassionate)
- Strong call to action

Retargeting for Long Decision Cycles

Divorce decisions take time. Potential clients often research for weeks before hiring. Retargeting keeps your firm visible:

- Show display ads to people who visited your site
- Vary messaging (educational content, testimonials, consultation offers)
- Exclude converters from retargeting audiences
- Set appropriate frequency caps (don't feel stalker-ish)

Reputation Management for Family Attorneys

Handling Sensitive Reviews

Family law reviews present unique challenges. Clients may be emotional, outcomes are often mixed (both parties rarely feel like winners), and confidentiality is critical.

Review Response Guidelines:
- Respond professionally to all reviews, positive and negative
- Never reveal client details or case information
- Acknowledge their feelings without being defensive
- Offer to discuss offline if there are concerns
- Thank positive reviewers specifically

Handling Negative Reviews:
- Don't argue facts in public
- Maintain confidentiality even if the reviewer doesn't
- Focus on process and your commitment to clients
- Report reviews that violate platform policies (if applicable)

Building a Review Generation System

Proactively building positive reviews offsets occasional negatives:

- Ask for reviews at case conclusion when outcomes are favorable
- Make it easy—provide direct links to your Google profile
- Train staff to identify satisfied clients for review requests
- Follow up once, don't badger
- Thank reviewers personally
